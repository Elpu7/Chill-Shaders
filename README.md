## About

Chill Shaders enhances Minecraft’s familiar visual style with atmospheric lighting, soft shadows, reflective water and distinctive environments.

The pack is built around a clean vanilla-inspired look. Blocks, textures, colours and Minecraft’s original character remain intact while lighting and atmosphere receive a polished upgrade.

## Features

- Soft, stable sunlight and moonlight shadows
- Reflective animated water with underwater lighting and fog
- Weather- and time-aware skies and clouds
- Brighter stars and improved night-time atmosphere
- Reflective ice and glowing lava, fire and soul fire
- Warm, biome-aware Nether fog and dense lava fog
- A custom animated End sky with stars and subtle purple space fog
- Natural vegetation movement
- Preserved entity, armour and coloured-text rendering
- Potato, Chill and High quality profiles

## Performance

Chill Shaders includes three selectable profiles:

- **Potato** – Lightweight shadows and simplified effects for maximum performance
- **Chill** – The recommended balance between visuals and frame rate
- **High** – Higher-resolution shadows, enhanced reflections and richer atmospheric effects

Individual options for shadows, water, fog, vegetation and post-processing can also be adjusted separately.

## Requirements

- `Iris 1.6.4 + 1.20+` – Recommended
- `OptiFine` – Supported
